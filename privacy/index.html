Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Victoria's Auto Repair ("we") handles when you visit this website or contact us through it.

Who we are

Victoria's Auto Repair, 1532 National City Blvd, National City, CA 91950. Phone: (619) 773-6087.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, user accounts, analytics or advertising cookies. We don't collect personal information through the site itself.

[CONFIRMAR: actualizar esta sección si se agrega analítica (por ejemplo Google Analytics) o el agente de citas por WhatsApp/SMS]

When you call or text us

The "Book an appointment" button opens your phone's messaging app with a pre-written text to our number. The "Call" links open your phone app. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

When you contact us, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, vehicle, the problem and any dealer quote. We use it only to answer you, schedule your appointment and service your vehicle. We don't sell your personal information.

Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

Third-party services

Some services used by this website receive technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser connects to them:

  • Website hosting (GitHub Pages) keeps standard server logs. GitHub privacy statement
  • Google Fonts delivers the typeface used on this site. Google privacy policy
  • Unsplash serves some of the photos on this site. Unsplash privacy policy [CONFIRMAR: quitar cuando se reemplacen por fotos propias]
  • Google Maps opens only if you click "Directions."

Their own privacy policies apply to the information they handle.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

To ask what information we have from your messages or calls, or to ask us to delete it, call or text us at (619) 773-6087. Some records, such as repair invoices, may need to be kept as required by California law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
