Know your Mercedes-Benz

Service A or Service B on your dashboard?

Your car's Flexible Service System alternates between two maintenance visits and tells you which one is due. Here is what each usually includes, so you know what you're paying for.

Service AUsually around 1 year or 10,000 miles

Synthetic oil and filter, fluid levels, tire pressure, brake inspection and maintenance counter reset.

Service BUsually around 2 years or 20,000 miles

Everything in Service A, plus cabin filter replacement and a brake fluid exchange.

Airmatic air suspensionA known Mercedes-Benz issue

If your Mercedes sits lower than normal, especially after being parked, the air suspension may be losing pressure. Have it checked before it gets worse.

Sources: Mercedes-Benz of Laguna Niguel: Service A vs. Service B · RepairPal: Airmatic strut failure

Your warranty

Will an independent shop void my Mercedes-Benz warranty?

No. The FTC says a manufacturer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer worked on your car. Keep your receipts and follow the maintenance schedule. Source: FTC
