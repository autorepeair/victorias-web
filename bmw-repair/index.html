Know your BMW

Your BMW tells you what it needs.

BMW doesn't use a fixed schedule. Condition Based Service tracks wear and shows what's due on the dashboard. These are the items it watches and the issues BMW owners see most.

Condition Based ServiceWhat your dashboard tracks

Engine oil, brake pads, brake fluid (every 24 months), cabin filter and spark plugs. A yellow symbol means that service is due.

Oil leaksOne of the most common BMW issues

Oil filter housing gaskets and valve cover gaskets are frequent leak points.

Cooling systemWater pump and thermostat

Water pumps and thermostats can fail. Overheating or a coolant warning is the sign to have it checked.

Sources: RepairPal: BMW maintenance and CBS · Jerry: Common problems with BMWs

Your warranty

Will an independent shop void my BMW warranty?

No. The FTC says a manufacturer can't deny warranty coverage just because someone other than the dealer worked on your car. Keep your receipts and follow the maintenance schedule. Source: FTC
